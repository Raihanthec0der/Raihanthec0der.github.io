Short reflections, each tied to a real finding above.

From: Sales-Profitability-Analysis

A discount isn't free

Discount rate and profit on Tables came out to roughly −0.667 correlation. It's a reminder that "more discount, more volume" doesn't automatically mean "more profit" — worth checking before recommending a promotion.

From: Student-performance-analysis

The best predictor was already in the data

G2 (second-period grade) correlated with the final grade at 0.905 — stronger than failures, absences, or parental education combined. Sometimes the strongest feature is just "what happened most recently."

From: sales-data-cleaning-analysis

Cleaning takes longer than charting

Most of the actual time on that project went into handling missing values and type conversions — the visualization step at the end was the fast part. I now budget time accordingly.
