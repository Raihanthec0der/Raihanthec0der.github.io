About Me

My background in mathematics gave me a strong foundation in logic, patterns, and methodical problem-solving, which sparked my fascination with data. Over time, that foundation evolved into a passion for transforming data into meaningful insights and knowledge.

I am currently expanding my expertise in Data Analytics, Data Science, and Artificial Intelligence, working with Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy, Excel, and Power BI. I enjoy exploring complex datasets, uncovering patterns, and translating analytical findings into clear, practical solutions.

I believe data science is more than technology; it is a way of thinking. It means asking better questions, challenging assumptions, and making decisions grounded in evidence.

As I continue learning and building real-world projects, my long-term goal is to create innovative, data-driven solutions with measurable impact.
